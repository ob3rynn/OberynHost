# Customer Responsibility

OberynHost servers are self-managed unless a future product explicitly says otherwise.

Customers manage:

- plugin installation, configuration, and troubleshooting,
- plugin conflicts and plugin-caused crashes,
- modpacks or modded servers unless sold later as a supported product,
- datapack behavior,
- player moderation,
- OP and whitelist policy decisions,
- gameplay disputes,
- custom jars,
- worlds broken by customer action,
- `server.properties` and custom configuration mistakes,
- workload-caused performance issues such as excessive entities, farms, redstone, view distance, player load, or heavy plugins.

OberynHost may help identify whether an issue appears platform-related, but customer-managed server administration remains the customer's responsibility.
